🎥 Translation Animation

This is an animation of the translation process of the structure 6C2U from the PDB. It is an ATP-binding protein containing two P-loop motifs.
Each frame is an ESMFold structure prediction after a step of translation. Red residues are late, and blue are early.

The second animation shows the ESMFold prediction of the translated sequence superimposed onto the original structure.
